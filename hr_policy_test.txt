CONFIDENTIAL HR POLICY DOCUMENT
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Department: Human Resources (HR)

1. Employee Remote Work Guidelines
All employees are entitled to 3 days of remote work per week. Remote work days must be approved by the direct manager at least 48 hours in advance. Employees must be available via Slack and email during core working hours (10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST).

2. PTO (Paid Time Off) Allowances
Employees accrue 1.5 days of PTO per month. A maximum of 5 unused PTO days can be rolled over to the next calendar year.

3. Expense Reimbursements
All travel expenses exceeding $50 must be accompanied by an itemized receipt. Food expenses are capped at $75 per diem during official corporate travel.

End of Document.
